### `calfire.harvest.proposed`

**CAL FIRE** (first party) · weekly · active

Proposed timber harvest plans. PLAN_STAT is a three-state pipeline — Proposed, Withdrawn, Denied — and withdrawn plans do not appear in CAL FIRE's permanent 76,967-record THP archive (20 sampled by HD_NUM, none found). But this layer is NOT a clean current-state snapshot: it holds a live pipeline (141 of 149 Proposed were filed 2025-26) alongside a retained withdrawal back-catalogue reaching filing year 2015. Any ratio of the two is meaningless. COMMENTS is the field that matters and was missed on the first build: populated on 88% of withdrawn plans, it carries the withdrawal date, the reason, and — on a third of them — the number the plan was resubmitted under, so a withdrawal is often a refiling rather than a death. Geometry is generalised to ~22 m so maps are possible without the archive carrying full-resolution polygons. Unlike the permanent THP layer, this one has no LANDOWNER or TIMBEROWNR field, so no personal data.
